This is the first entry in **AI Trail** — a public-ish journal of how
I'm using AI in my day-to-day building. Not a tutorial site. Not a
think-piece site. Just notes from the trail.

## Why log it

A few reasons:

1. **Memory is cheap when written down.** I keep rediscovering the same
   patterns. Writing them down means I stop paying that tax.
2. **AI tooling moves fast.** What worked last month might be obsolete
   next month. A dated journal beats a polished evergreen page.
3. **Showing my working** is more useful than showing my conclusions —
   to future-me, and to anyone walking the same trail behind me.

## What you'll find here

- Project logs from things I'm shipping
- Patterns I keep reaching for (and ones I keep regretting)
- Honest notes when something doesn't work

> Trees grow slow. So does taste. This is a place for both.

See you on the next entry.
